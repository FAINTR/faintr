How the grades work

Every battle runs on PvPoke's own simulator, on your computer. The four PvPoke grades are computed exactly the way PvPoke's Team Builder does it with default settings, so a team entered on pvpoke.com in the order shown here gets the same letters. The six FAINTR team checks are our own, scored against every team in the same league or cup: the best 10% earn an A, the next 20% a B, the next 30% a C, the next 25% a D, and the bottom 15% an F. Meta Wins plays each team in simulated 3v3s against the 60 strongest varied teams in the league, so the overall score rewards teams that actually win, not just teams with nice numbers. The overall score is colored by where it ranks among all teams: top 0.1%, 1%, 5% and 25%.

Anti-Meta (the button next to the leagues) ranks every team purely by how often it beats those 60 teams. The top 200 are re-played as 3v3 chains on PvPoke's own battle engine, with HP, energy and shields carried between fights.

Data and updates

FAINTR's website updates itself every night at midnight Central time: it checks PvPoke for new rankings and moves, re-runs every matchup that changed and publishes the results. You don't need to do anything.

Dev tools

Run the nightly update now. "Check now" rebuilds only what changed at PvPoke; "Rebuild everything" re-runs every league and cup (about an hour).

Time

Downloads PvPoke's latest game data and rankings from GitHub, including new cups. If anything changed, FAINTR re-runs every matchup and re-scores every team on this computer. Your current data stays in place until it finishes.
