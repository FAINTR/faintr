# What's New

## October 22, 2026 — Your Roster, your leagues

**Choose where each Pokémon belongs.** Your Great League Pokémon should not appear in Ultra League tools unless you want to build them for Ultra.

- **Set your league checkboxes in Roster.** Each Pokémon has Little, Great, Ultra and Master choices. Tick one or several leagues to include it there, or untick a league to leave it out. You can also change these choices when adding or editing a Pokémon.
- **Keep team building focused.** Your choices apply to the Team Grader's Roster dropdown, “Only Pokémon in my Roster” team searches, Roster-based counters and build planning. A Mimikyu marked Great only stays out of your Ultra Roster results—there is no assumption that you want to spend XL Candy on it. If you later plan an Ultra build, tick Ultra too.
- **Current CP still matters.** A Pokémon already above a league's CP cap is excluded from that league's Roster tools, even if its checkbox was previously selected. A 3,737 CP Metagross cannot appear as a Great League Roster option.
- **Cups follow their CP league.** Fantasy Cup uses your Great League choices because its cap is 1,500 CP. The cup's own species and type restrictions still apply. The same approach works for Little, Ultra and Master cups.

Existing Pokémon with a recorded CP start in the nearest matching CP league. Entries without CP keep all league choices until you set them. Review the checkboxes to match what you actually want to build.

**Suggested by Discord member scavenger.** Thank you for the idea to organize the Roster with league checkboxes!

## October 22, 2026 — Easier to browse

- **Teams first on phones.** Open Filters when you need them. See how many settings are active, then tap Show teams to return to the results.
- **Team details at a glance.** Read the full Pokémon names and moves, grade badges and overall score directly on each team card. Expand a team for matchup details and grade explanations.
- **Simpler Team Grader.** Pick a Pokémon to reveal its moves and IVs. Empty slots stay out of your way.

## October 22, 2026 — Share your team

- **Share straight from the team list.** Tap Share team, then Copy link.
- **Open the same setup.** Friends see the Pokémon, moves, IVs and league you shared. No account needed.
- **Try a replacement.** Choose the lead, safe swap or closer to change without rebuilding the whole team.
- **Download a team card.** Get a branded image to attach to Discord or a social post. Paste the team link with it so others can try the setup.
- **Previews for shared links.** Supported apps can show the team card automatically. Preview behavior depends on the app.

Your private roster, account details and notes are never included. Team results use the latest available game data, so grades can change after updates.
