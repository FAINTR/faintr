# What's New

## October 22, 2026 — Easier to browse

- **Teams first on phones.** Open Filters when you need them. See how many settings are active, then tap Show teams to return to the results.
- **Cleaner team cards.** See the Pokémon and score first. Expand Grades and checks for the full labels and a plain-English explanation.
- **Simpler Team Grader.** Pick a Pokémon to reveal its moves and IVs. Empty slots stay out of your way.

## October 22, 2026 — Share your team

- **Share straight from the team list.** Tap Share team, then Copy link.
- **Open the same setup.** Friends see the Pokémon, moves, IVs and league you shared. No account needed.
- **Try a replacement.** Choose the lead, safe swap or closer to change without rebuilding the whole team.
- **Download a team card.** Get a branded image to attach to Discord or a social post. Paste the team link with it so others can try the setup.
- **Previews for shared links.** Supported apps can show the team card automatically. Preview behavior depends on the app.

Your private roster, account details and notes are never included. Team results use the latest available game data, so grades can change after updates.

- **Support link, easier to find.** Find Support FAINTR beside Data and updates, near the page heading.
